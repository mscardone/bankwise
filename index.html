    Advice

    Out of the box the advice uses only RuneScape Wiki data, so it is the same for everyone. The options below make it personal; all are off until you turn them on.

    RuneMetrics

    Only needed for the quest-log and skill-level options. The quest log needs a public RuneMetrics profile; skill levels work either way.
    Lookup blocked? Paste the data instead
    Open each address in your browser, copy everything, paste it here and press Add. Do both.

    Overlay

    What gets drawn on top of the bank. The overlay tick-box in the header switches all of it off at once.

    Wiki icons (guesses)

    The RuneScape Wiki has a picture of nearly every item. They are not pixel-for-pixel what the game draws, so an item recognised this way is a very good guess rather than a certainty: it is treated like any known item, and hovering it in the bank (with teach on) settles or corrects it. The app ships with the icons already built; rebuilding fetches every item icon from the wiki again and takes a while.
